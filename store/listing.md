## Connect IQ Store: WristOTP (paid)

- **App id:** `5bdabd0de07e48e1a7c103ee7a3d006c`
- **Package:** `watch/bin/store/WristOTP.iq`
- **Type:** Watch app
- **Category:** Tools

**Description**

Your two-factor codes on your wrist, alongside Google Authenticator.

Requires the free WristOTP phone app (iPhone or Android) to send your accounts to the watch.

WristOTP shows the same 6- and 8-digit TOTP codes as Google Authenticator, with a countdown ring, so you can sign in without reaching for your phone.

- Up to 50 accounts
- Optional PIN lock with automatic lockout after repeated wrong tries
- Glance shows your first code at a glance (hidden while the PIN is on)
- Works fully offline once synced: codes are computed on the watch

How to set it up: install the free WristOTP companion app on your iPhone or Android phone. In Google Authenticator, choose Transfer accounts → Export accounts, scan the QR code with WristOTP, and tap Send. Google Authenticator keeps working as before.

Supports TOTP with SHA-1 and SHA-256. SHA-512 and counter-based (HOTP) accounts are listed in the phone app as not supported.

**Free version:** try WristOTP Free first. It holds one account.

**Refunds** follow the Connect IQ Store policy (within 48 hours of purchase).

- **Keywords (App Store, 100 chars):** `2fa,otp,totp,authenticator,watch,wearable,two factor,codes,security,mfa,wrist,offline`

**Description**

WristOTP is the companion app for the WristOTP watch app on Garmin Connect IQ watches. It copies the accounts from Google Authenticator to your watch, so you can read your two-factor codes on your wrist.

- Scan Google Authenticator's export QR codes, or import a screenshot or photo of them
- Send them to your watch over Bluetooth, through Garmin Connect
- Optionally require a PIN on the watch

Private by design: no account, no servers, no analytics, no data collected. Nothing is saved on the phone; your secrets go only from this phone to your watch.

Requires a Connect IQ compatible Garmin watch, the Garmin Connect app, and WristOTP or WristOTP Free installed on the watch from the Connect IQ Store.
